The Blood of Dawnwalker Release Date, Unlock Times & Post-Launch Roadmap Status

Category: Live Stats & Roadmap • Updated: August 2026
⚡ The Short Answer: The Blood of Dawnwalker unlocks globally on PC at 22:00 UTC on September 2, 2026 — which is 11 PM in London and midnight September 3 in Central Europe. Consoles launch at midnight local time on September 3 (the Americas unlock together at midnight Eastern). Preload went live 48 hours before unlock on Steam and consoles, and the day-one patch adds a 60 FPS Performance Mode on PS5, PS5 Pro, and Xbox Series X.

The Date, Exactly As Documented

Two authoritative dates circulate, and both are correct in their own frame: the Steam store page displays Sep 2, 2026, while Bandai Namco's official game hub advertises September 3, 2026. This pattern indicates regional unlock timing rolling across time zones — when a storefront's local midnight arrives relative to a global release moment. The practical takeaway: your launcher decides your minute; the countdown on YOUR storefront is the binding one.

Unlock Times: The Exact Schedule (Official)

Rebel Wolves published the global release timings, and they reconcile the two dates everyone has been arguing about: the Steam countdown (Sep 2) shows the PC unlock moment, while Bandai Namco's marketing date (September 3) is the console rollout. Here is the schedule as announced:

PC (Steam / Xbox PC / GOG) — one global moment: the game unlocks at 22:00 UTC on September 2, 2026. In local terms: 11:00 PM BST in the UK, midnight on September 3 in Central Europe (CEST), 6:00 PM ET / 3:00 PM PT on September 2 in the Americas, and 7:00 AM KST / 8:00 AM AEST on September 3 in Asia-Pacific.

Consoles (PS5, Xbox Series X|S) — midnight local, September 3: each region unlocks at 00:00 local time. The exception is the Americas, where all console regions unlock together at midnight Eastern Time on September 3 — which is 9:00 PM PT / 10:00 PM MT / 11:00 PM CT on September 2.

Preload: available on Steam and consoles 48 hours before your platform's unlock — install the full game in advance and it activates at unlock. The download size has not been formally itemized by the publisher; the store page's 60 GB storage requirement is the working figure.

Practical takeaway: PC players in the Americas play on the evening of September 2; console players in the Americas play at midnight Eastern; everyone else plays at their local midnight going into September 3. The Steam countdown timer is the authoritative PC clock.

Where You Can Buy It at Launch

PC arrives via multiple storefronts — Steam plus Xbox-on-PC and GOG per Wikipedia's consolidated release table — alongside PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S boxes. Notably this is a simultaneous multi-platform premiere, not a staggered one: no platform waits. Preorder tracks are already open through the official buy portal carrying the Sangoran Wayfarer's Armour Set preorder bonus across editions.

Day-1 Patch: What Ships On Arrival

Publisher messaging confirms the first download includes a Performance Mode targeting 60 FPS on PS5, PS5 Pro, and Xbox Series X. Factor download size and installation time into launch-evening plans. PC-side day-one specifics (pre-load timing, launch patch notes) remain unpublished and are tracked here as they surface.

Post-Launch Roadmap: Officially Unannounced

No DLC packs, expansion arcs, season structures, or update cadences have been announced by Rebel Wolves or Bandai Namco as of this writing. Guides claiming otherwise are extrapolating. Given the narrative-sandbox design and complete single-sitting story claims, expect the studio to speak about future content only after launch reception settles. This page updates the moment anything roadmap-shaped becomes official.
