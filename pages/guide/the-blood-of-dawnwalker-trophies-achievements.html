The Blood of Dawnwalker Trophies & Achievements — 46 Steam Achievements, Platinum Needs 2 Runs

Category: Guides & Walkthroughs • Updated: August 2026
⚡ The Short Answer: The Blood of Dawnwalker ships with 46 Steam achievements (25 visible, 21 hidden) worth 1,000 Gamerscore, and a 47-trophy PlayStation list per PowerPyx. Achievements span seven categories — exploration, leveling, combat and story among them — and the Platinum is mathematically a multi-run project: mutually exclusive choices lock some trophies behind decisions you cannot take twice in one playthrough.

The Numbers, Verified

Rebel Wolves revealed the full list ahead of launch (official Steam announcement): 46 Steam achievements — 25 visible and 21 hidden, 17 of them flagged secret — worth 1,000 Gamerscore on Xbox (TrueAchievements breakdown). The PlayStation side lists 47 trophies including the Platinum, per PowerPyx's full list.

Seven Categories, One Structural Catch

The achievements split across seven categories — visiting map locations, leveling, combat, story progression and more — which reads as a tour of the game's systems rather than a checklist of chores. The structural catch: because choices are mutually exclusive, the Platinum/all-achievements run cannot be done in a single playthrough. Plan at least two runs with opposite alignment on the big decisions.

Roadmap Logic for Trophy Hunters

  • Run 1 — play blind, pick your instincts. The story achievements and a large share of category trophies unlock naturally; hunting mid-run is what the hidden 21 punish.
  • Run 2 — the opposite mask. Take the major decisions you skipped, opposite day/night identity, cleanup per the Fextralife guide (which itemizes the hidden entries).
  • Expect patches to matter. Launch-window patches occasionally fix buggy achievements; our patch tracker follows official notes.

Frequently Asked Directives

How many achievements are in The Blood of Dawnwalker? ▼
46 on Steam (25 visible, 21 hidden, 17 secret) worth 1,000 Gamerscore, and 47 trophies on PlayStation including the Platinum.
Can you get the Platinum in one playthrough? ▼
No — mutually exclusive choices lock some trophies behind decisions, so the Platinum requires at least two playthroughs by design.
Are there hidden achievements? ▼
Yes — 17 achievements are flagged as secret on Steam, and PowerPyx and Fextralife maintain itemized lists including the hidden ones.
Do achievements require completionist time investment? ▼
Effectively yes: with 80–100+ hours across the multiple runs full completion demands, it is the game's longest project. A single story run unlocks only the story-side subset.
