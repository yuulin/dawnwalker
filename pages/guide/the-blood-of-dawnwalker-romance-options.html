The Blood of Dawnwalker Romance Options — Anca, Lacra & Crake

Category: Guides & Walkthroughs • Updated: August 2026
⚡ The Short Answer: Three romanceable characters: Anca, Lacra and Crake. Romance competes with the 30-day calendar.

The Three Characters

  • Anca — human/daylight; prologue start.
  • Lacra — vampire/night; early calendar window.
  • Crake — ally system; mid-game start.

Romance and the 30 Days

Romance questlines consume the same 30-day budget. Developers noted narrative consequences — it is a strategic investment.

One Path per Playthrough

Spreading across all three yields no romance. Focused attention on one character is required.
