The Blood of Dawnwalker Eclipse Edition ($79.99): Digital-Only Contents & Upgrade Logic

Author: The Blood of Dawnwalker Editorial Team • Category: Guides & Walkthroughs • Tier: FACTOID • Last Updated: August 2026
⚡ Bottom Line Up Front (BLUF): The Eclipse Edition is the digital premium tier at $79.99 — ten dollars over Standard — packing the full game plus a digital World Compendium, digital soundtrack, digital comic book, and a gold-laminated sticker set, sold through PlayStation's storefront per IGN's preorder documentation.

Confirmed Contents, Item By Item

IGN's preorder guide, cross-referencing the PlayStation Store listing, documents the Eclipse Edition as:

  • The Blood of Dawnwalker (full game)
  • Digital World Compendium
  • Digital Soundtrack
  • Digital Comic Book
  • Gold-laminated sticker set
Note the stack's all-digital constitution apart from the stickers — lore artifacts in downloadable form for players who want the world-building layer without shelf cargo.

Pricing Position Against Standard & Collector's

The ten-dollar gap above Standard ($69.99 → $79.99) buys purely supplemental media: soundtrack listening, comic reading, compendium browsing. Contrast with the $199.99 physical Collector's Edition whose figurine-and-hardcore reality cannot be transmitted digitally. Some aggregators list this tier under "Deluxe" labeling — our price guide documents the naming wrinkle transparently; the contents line at your checkout is the ground truth to honor.

Should YOU Buy It: Three Buyer Profiles

Profile fits: soundtrack devotees (dark-fantasy scores age beautifully in playlists), lore readers who prefer tablets to 60-page hardcovers, and completionist-minded players wanting every registered artifact. Profile mismatches: strict budgetarians (nothing here affects gameplay in a single-player RPG — zero power, zero quests, zero gating relief), shelf-collectors whose heart belongs to figurines, and patients comfortable waiting to hear the OST sampled publicly before committing. In edition-value terms it is the middle child: modest risk, modest premium, clear ledger.

PC Player Footnote

Eclipse terminology surfaces most concretely through the PlayStation storefront; PC-storefront equivalents and their exact naming may vary. The standing instruction mirrors our price page: whatever your storefront calls its $79.99 tier, verify the five-item contents list matches before paying, and treat bracket-label disputes as merchant housekeeping rather than player concern. PC-specific digital deluxe composition remains flagged officially pending until storefront pages consolidate.

Frequently Asked Directives

What is included in the Eclipse Edition? ▼
The full game plus a digital World Compendium, digital soundtrack, digital comic book, and a gold-laminated sticker set, per IGN's preorder documentation and the PlayStation Store listing.
How much does the Eclipse Edition cost? ▼
$79.99 — a ten-dollar step above the $69.99 Standard Edition.
Is the Eclipse Edition worth it over Standard? ▼
Only if you value the digital media stack (soundtrack, comic, compendium). None of the extras affect gameplay — it is pure supplementary media.
Why do some sites call it the Deluxe Edition? ▼
Roundups disagree on premium-tier labels while pricing converges at $79.99; storefront checkout contents are the definitive reference — the discrepancy is documented transparently in our price guide.
Do I get the preorder armour set with Eclipse? ▼
Yes — the Sangoran Wayfarer's Armour Set preorder bonus attaches across all editions per the official buy page.
