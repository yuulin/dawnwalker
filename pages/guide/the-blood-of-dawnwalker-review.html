The Blood of Dawnwalker Review Roundup — Metacritic 84, OpenCritic 86 & Why Scores Split

Category: Guides & Walkthroughs • Updated: August 2026
⚡ The Short Answer: The Blood of Dawnwalker launched to strong reviews: Metacritic 84, OpenCritic 86 ("Mighty") with 91% of critics recommending it across 50+ reviews — and it hit #1 on Steam's global top-sellers chart at launch. The interesting part is the spread: scores run from 5/10 to near-perfect, split over whether the 30-day time system is the game's brilliance or its flaw. Here is the full score picture and both sides of the argument.

The Scores, Verified

Aggregated from OpenCritic and the launch review threads (verified September 3, 2026):

  • Metacritic: 84/100 — which, as GamesRadar pointed out, puts it within touching distance of The Witcher's 86, a comparison that mattered to creative director Konrad Tomaszkiewicz given Rebel Wolves' ex-CDPR pedigree.
  • OpenCritic: 86 average, rated "Mighty" — with 91% of critics recommending the game across 50+ reviews, placing it in OpenCritic's 91st percentile.
  • Commercial signal: #1 on Steam's global top-sellers chart around launch, with roughly 37,000–43,000 concurrent players in the opening window.

What Critics Praise

  • The time-management system, mostly. Vice's review calls the timer "brilliant" precisely because it gives decisions real weight — you cannot do everything, so choices actually cost something. The Reddit launch consensus echoes it: "meaningful choices, a clever time-management system and satisfying combat."
  • A confident first game. RPG Site called it "an instant classic" and possibly their favorite RPG of 2026 — high praise for a studio's debut, even one staffed by veteran Witcher 3 developers.
  • Characters and writing. Reviewers repeatedly highlight the well-written cast alongside the systems praise.

What Critics Dinged — and Why Scores Split

The spread is real: individual scores run from around 5/10 to near-perfect, which is unusual for a well-received game and worth understanding before you buy.

  • The time system cuts both ways. The same mechanic Vice loves, Game Informer's review (tellingly titled "Time Doesn't Heal All Wounds") treats more skeptically — how consequences land over the 30 days did not work for every reviewer.
  • Debut rough edges. A first game from a new studio draws harsher framing on bugs and scope than an established franchise gets.
  • Expectation weight. Founded by ex-Witcher 3 developers, Rebel Wolves was always going to be measured against CDPR's best — some reviewers anchored to that bar.

Practical read for buyers: if a run-based structure where you cannot see everything in one playthrough sounds appealing, the positive camp describes your game. If you want a completable checklist RPG, the critical camp's complaints are about exactly that. Our time system explainer covers the mechanic in detail, and the beginner guide covers how to plan around it.

Launch Context: Sales and Players

The commercial launch backed the reviews: #1 on Steam's global top-sellers in launch week and roughly 37,000–43,000 concurrent players in the opening window (SteamDB listed ~37,340 during day-one peaks; our own API sampling caught 43,492). Console players get the day-one patch's Performance Mode targeting 60 FPS on PS5, PS5 Pro, and Xbox Series X — a late addition after fan backlash to the original 30 FPS plans; details on our day-one patch page.

Frequently Asked Directives

Is The Blood of Dawnwalker good? ▼
By the aggregate numbers, yes: Metacritic 84, OpenCritic 86 rated Mighty, and 91% of critics recommend it. The honest caveat is the spread — individual scores range from around 5/10 to near-perfect, split over how well the 30-day time system works for different players.
What did critics criticize in The Blood of Dawnwalker? ▼
The main sticking point is the time-management system — Game Informer's review is more skeptical about how consequences land, while other outlets call the same system brilliant. Reviewers also noted debut-game rough edges, and some anchored expectations to the founders' Witcher 3 pedigree.
How does Dawnwalker's score compare to The Witcher 3? ▼
The Witcher 3 sits at 86 on Metacritic; Dawnwalker launched at 84 — close enough that GamesRadar highlighted the comparison, which mattered given Rebel Wolves was founded by former Witcher 3 developers.
Did The Blood of Dawnwalker sell well? ▼
Yes — it reached #1 on Steam's global top-sellers chart around launch, with roughly 37,000-43,000 concurrent players in the opening window on Steam alone, before counting consoles.
