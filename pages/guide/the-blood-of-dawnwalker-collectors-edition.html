The Blood of Dawnwalker Collector's Edition: Full Contents, Price & Should You Order It

Category: Guides & Walkthroughs • Updated: August 2026
⚡ The Short Answer: The Collector's Edition is the physicalpremium box at $199.99 / £179.99 / €199.99, containing the full game, a Steelbook case, a 23 cm World Box, a 60-page physical World Compendium hardcover, and a Coen figurine — per Bandai Namco's own store listing.

Everything In The Box (From The Publisher's Own Listing)

The official Bandai Namco EU store enumerates the package without ambiguity:

  • The Blood of Dawnwalker base game
  • Steelbook® metal case
  • World Box (23 cm)
  • Physical World Compendium — 60-page hardcover
  • Coen figurine presented in a collector box
That last piece is the headline object: the hero rendered sculpturally, sized at the box-display scale serious shelf collectors expect.

What It Costs And Where It Lives

Anchors put it at $199.99 US, £179.99 UK, €199.99 EUR — triple-digit pricing territory reserved for the memorial-goods tier. Distribution flows through the Bandai Namco store network as a physical SKU (the EU listing presents the PS5 full-game configuration), with retailer availability varying by region. Digital-only buyers looking at this box should understand its center of gravity is the artifact stack, not additional software entitlements.

Collector Math: Who Gets Value Here

Break the $199.99 against components: the game alone carries the $69.99 standard value; everything above it — steelbook, figurine, hardcover, display goods — prices the remainder around $130. Fans of 14th-century dark-fantasy worldbuilding get genuine reading material (the compendium) rather than empty filler cards. Apartment-space pragmatists and digital-library loyalists gain little; the edition's soul is physical presence. If the vampire-court aesthetic already owns a shelf corner in your home, this was engineered precisely for it.

Ordering Notes Worth Knowing Before Checkout

The official buy portal confirms the Sangoran Wayfarer's Armour Set preorder bonus rides along with the Collector's Edition like every other tier. Region-specific shipping timelines and SKU formats differ per storefront — validate destination compatibility at the point of sale. The box's game code region/edition pairing should be checked against your console's storefront account before committing overseas orders.
