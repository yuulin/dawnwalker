The Blood of Dawnwalker Price Guide: Every Edition, Regional Pricing & Preorder Bonus

Category: Guides & Walkthroughs • Updated: August 2026
⚡ The Short Answer: Anchors from current retail data: Standard Edition $69.99 / £59.99 / €69.99; the premium digital tier sits at $79.99 (sold as Eclipse on PlayStation storefronts, with Deluxe naming appearing in some roundups — treat storefront labels as governing); the physical-only Collector's Edition lands at $199.99 / £179.99 / €199.99. Every edition carries the Sangoran Wayfarer's Armour Set preorder bonus.

United States Dollar Anchors

Compiled from Insider-Gaming's edition breakdown and cross-checked against store listings: Standard $69.99, premium digital tier $79.99 (see naming note below), and Collector's $199.99. Some big-box physical listings surface near-$59.99 price points for console standard copies during promotional framing — those are retailer-specific presentations of the standard package, useful for bargain watchers but not the publisher anchor. Rule of thumb: judge value off official storefront counts, seize retailer dips opportunistically.

UK & Euro Pricing

UK windows track dollar structure closely — £59.99 standard, £69.99 premium tier, £179.99 collector's per the same roundup. On the continent the Bandai Namco EU store pegs Standard from €69.99 and the Collector's from €199.99. European collectors should also note shipping/logistics wording differs per SKU when ordering physical premium bundles.

The Deluxe-vs-Eclipse Naming Wrinkle (Explained Honestly)

Two labels describe overlapping premium offers and mismatched roundup prose creates confusion: PlayStation's digital storefront sells the Eclipse Edition at $79.99 (verified via Sony listing linked from IGN's preorder guide: digital compendium, soundtrack, comic book, gold-laminated sticker set), while some aggregator tables print "Deluxe $79.99" for equivalent positioning. Our interpretation pending publisher clarification: same commercial tier, storefront-dependent presentation. We label discrepancies openly rather than flattening them — verify the contents line at checkout before paying either name.

Value Logic: Which Spend Matches Which Player

Pure story seekers lose nothing choosing Standard — cosmetics and lore paraphernalia don't gate systems in a single-player RPG (no gameplay-power extras are advertised in any edition's contents). Lore devotees extracting world-bible enjoyment leap straight to premium digital add-ons. Physical memorial-object collectors already know the answer is the figurine-bearing Collector's box, priced triple-digit with steelbook and world map goods included — details unpacked in our dedicated edition pages.
