Rebel Wolves — The Ex-CDPR Studio Behind Dawnwalker

Category: Guides & Walkthroughs • Updated: August 2026
⚡ The Short Answer: Founded 2019 by ex-CD Projekt Red developers including Konrad Tomaszkiewicz. Debut Dawnwalker scored Metacritic 84, Steam #1.

Who Is Rebel Wolves?

Founded 2019 by ex-CDPR developers including Konrad Tomaszkiewicz. Based in Poland. Dawnwalker is their debut, published by Bandai Namco.

CDPR Connection

Multiple senior devs worked on The Witcher 3. Dawnwalker is a new IP, not a Witcher game.

Future

MC 84 + Steam #1 debut. Follow the developer page.
