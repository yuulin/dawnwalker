The Blood of Dawnwalker System Requirements: Can Your PC Run It? (Minimum vs Recommended)

Category: Technical & Optimization • Updated: August 2026
⚡ The Short Answer: If your PC pairs a GTX 1060 / RX 580 with an Intel Core i5-11400F or Ryzen 7 2700X and 16 GB of RAM, you meet the official minimum spec. The recommended bar is an RTX 4060 / RX 7600 XT / Arc B580 with an i7-11700K or Ryzen 7 5700X. Everyone needs 60 GB on an SSD and Windows 10 with DirectX 12.

Official Minimum Requirements (1080p Entry Point)

These are the exact PC specifications published by publisher Bandai Namco Entertainment on the game's official Steam store page:

  • OS: Windows 10, DirectX 12 (64-bit)
  • CPU: Intel Core i5-11400F or AMD Ryzen 7 2700X
  • RAM: 16 GB
  • GPU: NVIDIA GTX 1060 or Radeon RX 580 — 6 GB VRAM explicitly required
  • Storage: 60 GB available space, SSD mandatory per the additional notes

The specification sheet does not attach a resolution or framerate target to this tier. Based on how these parts are positioned by the publisher, treat the minimum tier as the "launches and plays" bar rather than a quality promise.

Official Recommended Requirements

The recommended tier steps up every pillar while keeping RAM unchanged:

  • CPU: Intel Core i7-11700K or AMD Ryzen 7 5700X
  • GPU: NVIDIA RTX 4060, Radeon RX 7600 XT, or Intel Arc B580 — 8 GB VRAM listed
  • RAM: 16 GB (same as minimum — memory is not the differentiator)
  • Storage / OS: identical 60 GB SSD requirement, Windows 10 DirectX 12

Notably, an Intel Arc B580 appears among the recommended cards, which signals the studio validated modern GPU feature sets beyond the usual NVIDIA/AMD pairing.

What the Specs Tell You Before Launch

Three planning takeaways drawn strictly from the published data: (1) 16 GB RAM is a hard floor for both tiers — 8 GB machines are officially out of scope. (2) An SSD is not optional; both tiers repeat the SSD note, consistent with other current-generation open-world RPGs that stream world assets continuously. (3) The VRAM jump from 6 GB to 8 GB marks the recommended boundary, making texture headroom the most likely lever between tiers.

Actual performance (fps presets, upscaling behavior) has not been published by Rebel Wolves yet; any per-preset numbers circulating before launch should be treated as speculation. This page will be updated once benchmark data exists.

Fatal Pitfalls & Avoidance Directives

⚠️

Assuming 8 GB RAM is survivable

16 GB is printed on BOTH official tiers. Open-world RPGs stream assets aggressively; staying under spec risks stutter well below any graphics discussion.

⚠️

Installing on a spinning HDD

The additional notes call out SSD storage twice (once per tier). An HDD install violates the letter of the requirements, not just the spirit.

⚠️

Buying a 4 GB VRAM card for entry

The minimum line item itself specifies 6 GB VRAM. Older 4 GB variants of similarly-named cards (e.g., GTX 1060 3GB) fail the written requirement.

⚠️

Trusting pre-launch fps claims

No official preset-to-framerate mapping exists yet. Treat any benchmark guarantee made before independent reviews as marketing noise.

Frequently Asked Directives

Can The Blood of Dawnwalker run on 8 GB of RAM? ▼
No. Both the minimum and recommended specifications list 16 GB RAM on the official Steam page, so 8 GB systems fall outside supported configuration.
Is an HDD acceptable since SSDs are only in the notes? ▼
Practically no — the additional notes section makes SSD storage explicit for both tiers, and open-world asset streaming on current-generation RPGs assumes SSD latency.
Does the recommended spec mean 60 FPS? ▼
The store page does not bind any framerate to either tier. Preset-to-framerate expectations remain unverified until post-launch benchmarks.
Is Windows 11 supported? ▼
Windows 10 with DirectX 12 is the listed requirement; DirectX 12 titles run on Windows 11, but the official documentation names Windows 10.
