The Blood of Dawnwalker Difficulty Levels — Story, Fair, Challenging & Duelist

Category: Game Modes & Difficulty • Updated: August 2026
⚡ The Short Answer: Four presets: Story (slowed animations), Fair (intended), Challenging (veterans), Duelist (maximum). Does not interact with the 30-day calendar.

The Four Presets

PresetBest for
StoryNarrative-first, slowed animations
FairIntended experience
ChallengingVeterans
DuelistMaximum challenge

Which to Pick

  • New: Story for learning directional combat.
  • Veteran: Fair or Challenging.
  • Replaying: Any — the 30-day system is the same.

Difficulty and the Calendar

Difficulty does not interact with the 30-day system. Harder modes stretch combat encounters.
